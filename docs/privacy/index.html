Vamoose Privacy Policy

Vamoose is a puzzle game for Android made by Jahangir Alisoy. This policy explains what information the app uses, who receives it and the choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account or sign-in. I do not run servers and I do not collect your personal information.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • If you buy Remove ads, Google Play handles the payment.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your phone only. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

  • Level progress and stars
  • Your hint count and the date of your last free daily hint
  • Daily puzzle days and your streak
  • Settings: easy mode, bigger text, sound effects and vibration
  • Whether you bought Remove ads

Settings › Reset progress clears your levels, stars and streak. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything above.

Advertising with Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, full-screen and optional reward ads. To show and measure ads, analyse their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location
  • Interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad loads. Ads that use personal data are shown only with your consent.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings, under Privacy › Ads or Google › Ads.
  • You can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at My Ad Center.
  • Buying Remove ads turns off banner and full-screen ads. Reward videos you choose to watch for a hint still come from AdMob.

Purchases with Google Play

Remove ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. I never see your payment details. Google Play confirms the purchase to the app on your phone, and the app remembers it there so ads stay off. Restore purchase asks Google Play for that confirmation again.

Children

Vamoose is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help remove it.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on my side to access, correct or delete. Data collected by Google is handled under Google's policies and the tools linked above. If you have any question or request about your privacy, write to me and I will reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
