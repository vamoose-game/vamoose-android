## Short description

Tap, slide, clear the board before the clock runs out. 360 levels.

## Full description

Every block has an arrow. Tap it and it slides that way. A clear path sends it off the board; a block in the way means a bump, and every bump costs a star. Clear the board before the clock runs out.

• 360 levels in 12 chapters, boards from 6×6 to 8×8
• A clock on every level, from 12 seconds up, tuned so every level is a race you can win
• Three stars per level: keep them by not bumping
• Locked blocks: clear the block with the key to open the lock
• Turning blocks: round blocks that turn every time another block leaves
• Frozen blocks: ice holds them until a block next to them leaves
• Twin blocks: chained pairs that leave together, so clear both ways first
• Countdown blocks: every block that leaves counts them down, so get them out in time
• Lives: a failed level costs one, and they come back over time
• Lives for coins or a short video, extra time for coins, hints for either
• Daily streak: collect coins 7 days in a row, open the day-7 chest and race a new daily puzzle
• Three board themes: Classic, Wood and Night
• Background music, sound effects and vibration you can switch off
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Remove ads with a one-time purchase

## First release notes

First test build of Vamoose.

## Release notes, 1.0.0 (9)

New: coins, a daily streak with a day-7 chest, and two board themes, Wood and Night. Up to three locked-block pairs, fuller first levels and better fit on small phones.

## Release notes, 1.1.0 (10)

Beat the clock: every level now has a timer and three stars to keep. New turning blocks from level 21, the first lock at level 11, harder levels from the start, lives, extra time and a store.

## Release notes, 1.2.0 (11)

New: countdown blocks from level 26. Every block that leaves counts them down; get them out before they reach 0, or lose a star. Up to three on a level, and the order matters. Levels with countdown blocks are a little harder; every one can be saved, and the hint shows the way.

## Release notes, 1.3.0 (12)

New: frozen blocks and twin blocks. Mechanics now arrive one at a time: locked blocks at level 11, turning blocks at 51, frozen blocks at 91, twin blocks at 131 and countdown blocks at 171, and later levels mix them all. Every level also has 2 more seconds on the clock.

## Release notes, 1.3.0 (13)

Frozen blocks crack: no more numbers on the ice. Each block that leaves next to a frozen one cracks it further until it shatters, and thicker ice looks frostier. Thick ice comes in at level 201, with a new intro, and the thickest ice, three layers, at 281.
