## Short description

Tap a block, slide it off the board. 360 calm puzzles, and you never get stuck.

## Full description

Vamoose is the calm block puzzle. Every block has an arrow. Tap it and it slides that way. If the path is clear, it leaves the board. If another block is in the way, it bumps and the block in the way lights up. Clear the board with no bumps to earn three stars.

There are no timers and no lives. Every level can be solved, so you can never get stuck.

• 360 levels in 12 chapters, from Warm-up to Zen
• Boards grow from 5×5 to 8×8 as you play
• A new daily puzzle every day, with streaks
• Hints show you a block that can go, plus one free hint every day
• Easy mode fades the blocks that cannot move yet
• Bigger text, sound effects and vibration you can switch on or off
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Remove ads with a one-time purchase
