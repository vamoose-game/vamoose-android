# Levels and timers

Made by `dart run tool/generate_levels.dart`. 400 simulated players (`lib/domain/pace`) play each level with the practice of the levels before it. A pass is a clear before the clock ends and before a third miss: a bump, or a countdown block that runs out. Experts are the quickest 15 % of players. Each clock is the fewest seconds that are fair for its tier, plus 2 s. The extra-time column is the first-try pass with the extra-time booster, a fifth of the clock (2–5 s).

Mechanics: locks from level 11 (279 levels), turning blocks from level 51 (245 levels), frozen blocks from level 91 (168 levels) (thick ice on 96), twin blocks from level 131 (139 levels) and countdown blocks from level 171 (96 levels); 19 levels have all five. Every level clears in any order. Countdown numbers run 3–9, and every countdown level has an order of moves that saves them all. On those levels a first try loses 0.06 countdown blocks on average.

Every level but 9 takes the crowd at least as long to clear, with no clock, as the level with its number in build 9. Build 9 had no clock, and its board would not be fair at 10 s there, so level 9 takes 9.2 s against 9.7 s, on the hardest board fair at 10 s.
